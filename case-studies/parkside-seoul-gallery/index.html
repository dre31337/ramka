Parkside Seoul Gallery

Kohn Pedersen Fox Associates
Seoul, South Korea
2024
Brief

The sales gallery for KPF's Parkside Seoul — the room where the project's materials are met up close, before the towers are built.

We illustrated it for its surfaces: terrazzo underfoot, bronze and copper at the walls, stone and travertine in long vertical panels, all under warm, low light.

Closer than the masterplan, quieter than the plaza — the building at the scale of a hand.

The lounge

Past the reception, the gallery opens to a lounge along the glass — the garden and its blossom held just beyond.

A place to sit with the project, the city softening into trees.
