Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library

Henning Larsen
Medora, North Dakota
2020
Brief

Henning Larsen's proposal for the Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library, one of three finalist schemes for a site above the badlands at Medora, North Dakota.

The building is low and long, rammed earth striated like the bluffs it sits on, its roof planted back into the prairie so the landscape runs over the top of it.

We illustrated it as a place in weather rather than an object — the grass in summer, a storm coming across the plain, deep snow at the entrance — and animated three approaches through the same ground.

The badlands

The site does as much as the building. Grass, rock, weather — the library sits into it rather than above it, and the illustrations had to give the land its share of the frame.
