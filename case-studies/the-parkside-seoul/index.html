The Parkside Seoul

Kohn Pedersen Fox Associates
Seoul, South Korea
2025
Brief

KPF's The Parkside Seoul is a mixed-use quarter set into the slope below Namsan — a cluster of towers gathered around a pedestrian street.

We illustrated it through its public realm rather than its skyline: the life of the plaza between the buildings, terraced with planting and retail, read from the ground.

One place across a single day — a weekday morning, an afternoon festival, the fountains in summer, the lit street after dark.

After dark

The same street keeps working after the towers light up — the retail glowing at its base, the gardens lit from within.

We carried the illustrations into the evening and the rain, where the public rooms read brightest against the dark.
