Banpo Ecological Playscape

Snøhetta
Seoul, South Korea
2024
Brief

Snøhetta's Banpo Ecological Playscape is a single continuous yellow line threading the park along the Han River in Seoul.

It rises over the expressway as a land bridge, reconnecting the Banpo neighbourhood with the water, and along the way swells into lookouts, slides and play — part path, part landscape, part instrument for the city.

We illustrated it from the river to the ground beneath the canopy — from above and at eye level, by day and at dusk.

Underneath

The path is a roof as much as a route — held on slender columns, it shelters the ground below and opens it to the planting.

Where it lifts, it becomes a slide; where it widens, a place to stand and look out over the water.
