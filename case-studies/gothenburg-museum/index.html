Gothenburg Museum of Art

JKMM
Gothenburg, Sweden
2024
Brief

JKMM's extension of the Gothenburg Museum of Art, illustrated for an invited international competition — a collaboration with Strategisk Arkitektur, their proposal 'Etos' selected among the top six.

The design reads as an excavation: the plinth of the annex cut away to open archaic, room-like spaces where the new meets the 1923 original without flattening either.

We framed it night and day — exteriors at the cardinal points, interiors in the light they'd have on a Tuesday in March.

Old and new

Between the 1923 building and the annex, a glazed gap opens the full height of the museum — the archaeological cut made legible, daylight falling the length of it.

The old museum keeps its character as a heritage landmark; the new annex turns the complex toward the city as a cultural destination.
